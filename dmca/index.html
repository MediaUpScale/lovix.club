DMCA Notice

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Lovix respects copyright and responds to valid notices under 17 U.S.C. § 512. Open a section below for what to send. This page is not legal advice.

Filing a takedown notice

Send a notice to dmca@lovix.club that includes:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  • The exact URL of the material on Lovix, specific enough for us to find it.
  • Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
Contact our DMCA agent

Designated agent: DMCA Agent, Lovix. Email dmca@lovix.club. A postal address will be published here before Lovix hosts material that requires a registered agent address.

Counter-notification

If you believe material was removed or disabled by mistake, you may send a counter-notification to dmca@lovix.club that includes your signature, the material that was removed and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the removal was a mistake, your name and contact details, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, if you are outside the United States, for any judicial district in which the operator may be found) and will accept service of process.

If the notice is valid, Lovix may restore the material after the statutory waiting period unless the original complainant files a court action.

Repeat infringers

Lovix will terminate access for repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances, including any upload or operator access that may be granted later.

Legal disclaimer

This page describes a process. It does not create an attorney-client relationship, and it is not a complete statement of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act. Consider your own counsel before you send a notice or a counter-notification.
